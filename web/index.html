What's inside

A whole city to fly

Downtown towers, twisting skyscrapers, Old Town rooftops, docks, factories, a park, a suspension bridge and an island prison fortress. Crowds chat on the sidewalks, gulls circle the harbor, and any car on the road can be lifted and thrown.

12-mission story in two acts

Stop the Cobalt Syndicate, then hunt Vesper Hale to her island fortress: convoys, a power-station siege, a courier tail, a bomb-defusal race on the bridge, a walker mech and a cloaking boss.

Abilities, upgrades and races

Shield, shockwave and dash on every suit. Earn Tech Points for six upgrade lines, and chase gold medals in four ring races, from street canyons to under the bridge deck.

Build anything

Six workbenches and your HQ, AEGIS Tower. Combine emitters, cells, lenses, mods and cooling into custom weapons, and plating, thrusters, reactors and helmets into custom suits.

JUNO, your co-pilot

A spoken AI companion who reacts to the fight, designs builds for you and tells you where to find any part.

Helmet HUD

First-person visor with suit diagnostics for every part, target lock and scan, threat arrows and a quest tracker.

Built to look good

Real-time shadows, rooms behind every window, wet reflective streets, sun rays, lens flare and 3D sound. Low, High and Ultra settings.

First time opening it? (unsigned app warnings)

Windows: if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.

macOS: right-click the app → Open → Open. If it says the app is damaged, run xattr -cr "/Applications/AEGIS Port Halcyon.app" in Terminal.

Linux: make the AppImage executable (chmod +x AEGIS-linux.AppImage) and run it.
